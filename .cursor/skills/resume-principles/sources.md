## Myths not to encode

- "75% of resumes are rejected by ATS": 2012 vendor marketing, no method published.
- "Bad formatting auto-rejects you": a failed parse leaves a half-filled profile. It still hurts search, so the parsing rules stay.
- "Recruiters read for 7.4 seconds": first skim only, one vendor study.
